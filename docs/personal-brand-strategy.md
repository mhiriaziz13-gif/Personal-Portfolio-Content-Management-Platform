## Brand system

Brand pillars: Marketing & Commercial Analytics; Business Intelligence and decision support; Big Data, AI and Digital Transformation; reliable automation; customer insight; hospitality and tourism context; cross-functional communication.

Proof points: published case studies; group-level connectivity and digital transformation responsibility at El Mouradi Hotels; verified tools and deliverables; professional timeline; education and credential records; public LinkedIn and GitHub profiles.

Audience: recruiters, hiring managers, analytics and automation teams, collaborators and professional communities. Target roles include Marketing & Commercial Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Marketing Data Analyst, CRM & Marketing Automation Analyst and Digital Transformation Analyst. Ahmed remains based in Sousse, Tunisia. He is open to selected freelance projects and building toward international full-time opportunities from 2027. This is career targeting, not a claim of residence, an office or employment authorization.

Tone: concrete, calm, evidence-led, first-hand and clear. Prefer analyze, reconcile, report, automate, validate and support decisions. Avoid guru, visionary, world-class, best-in-class, guaranteed growth and unsupported expert/senior claims. Always spell the name “Ahmed Aziz Mhiri” and keep LinkedIn/GitHub URLs consistent.

Checklist: identity spelling; positioning; tagline; profile URLs; location; availability; role family; first-hand evidence; confidentiality review; no invented metrics; no implied endorsements.
